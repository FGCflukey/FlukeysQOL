[!Server Restart Schedule]
The server restarts every day at 6:00 AM server time.
> Find somewhere safe a few minutes before the restart.

[Welcome to Starman's PvE Server]
This is a small private server for friends. Player damage is turned off, so it's just you, your friends and the zombies.

Press F9 at any time to see the rules and this page again.

[Server Features]
> Emergency Vendor
Right-click a vending machine to buy and sell items for cash.

> Chopper Travel
Buy a one-way chopper ticket at the Emergency Vendor to fast-travel to another town.

> Remove Hotwire
Sit in the driver's seat of a hotwired car with the engine off and use the radial menu.
